## Hey, we're Webready 👋

A small web development company from Kalmar, Sweden. We've been building websites, internal systems, apps and integrations since 2008 - design, development and hosting, usually for the long haul.

Most of what we do is client work and lives in private repositories. What you'll find here are the tools we built for ourselves, because we wanted something small enough to understand and own.

### Open source

- [**Puls**](https://github.com/webready-se/puls) - cookieless, lightweight analytics. One PHP file + SQLite.
- [**Vaka**](https://github.com/webready-se/vaka) - keeps watch while you sleep. Uptime, certificates, domains, exposed files, security headers and mail DNS for every site.

They share the same idea: self-hosted, PHP + SQLite, no dependencies, MIT licensed.

### Also from us

Not open source, but built and run by us:

- [**Snittränta**](https://xn--snittrnta-02a.se/) - compare average mortgage rates from 18 Swedish banks.
- [**PR LOG**](https://prlog.app/) - track your personal records in training.
